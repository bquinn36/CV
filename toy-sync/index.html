Funscripts are the standard timing format for scripted videos. Many PMVs have community scripts; drop the .funscript next to the video. Speed mode turns fast stroking into strong vibration and slow sections into gentle buzz.
No script needed. The video's own audio is analysed in-browser (nothing leaves your machine). Works best with PMVs because the edit is cut to the beat. Analysis only starts after you press play.
Tap along to the track, or set the BPM by hand. The pattern locks to the video clock, so pausing or scrubbing stays in time.

Shaping

Bluetooth toys lag 100–250 ms behind commands. Positive lead time sends commands early to compensate. Air pumps are slow mechanical things, so suction follows a smoothed version of the signal and changes at most a couple of times a second.

Intiface Central

Install Intiface Central (free, open source; desktop and Android), press its ▶ Start Server button, then connect here. Close the JoyHub app first: a toy can only hold one Bluetooth connection. On Android, keep this tab in the foreground while playing.
